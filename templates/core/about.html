Our FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What curricula do you cover?
    Mathematics and Computer Science across IB, Cambridge, AP, UK, US, Canadian, Australian, Singaporean, Indian and other major boards. See the Mathematics and Computer Science pages for the full list.
  • How are sessions scheduled across time zones?
    Sessions are scheduled directly with you after a discovery call, at a time that works for your student's time zone.
  • How do I get started?
    Book a free discovery call. We'll talk through your child's curriculum, level and goals, then agree on a plan, no obligation.
  • Is there a minimum commitment?
    No, pricing and packages are discussed on your discovery call so you can decide what fits your student. See the Pricing page for an overview.
  • Do you teach online, in person, or both?
    All sessions are conducted online over video call, so your student can join from anywhere with a stable internet connection.
  • What age or grade levels do you work with?
    Students from around ages 10 to 18, spanning upper primary through the end of secondary school and pre-university years.
  • Can you help with both Mathematics and Computer Science at once?
    Yes. Since both subjects are taught by the same teacher, we can cover both in a single ongoing plan instead of juggling two separate tutors.
  • Do you offer one-off sessions or only ongoing packages?
    Both. Some students book a single session before an exam, others prefer a regular weekly plan. We will figure out what fits on your discovery call.
